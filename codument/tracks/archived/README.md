# Archived Tracks

Completed, cancelled, or superseded tracks are retained here in `YYYY-MM/<timestamp>-<id>/` directories.

Historical tracks migrated from the former cozo-lib-dotnet repository are documented in [MIGRATED_FROM_COZO.md](MIGRATED_FROM_COZO.md). They are responsibility-scoped records, not unfiltered copies of the former monorepo tracks.
